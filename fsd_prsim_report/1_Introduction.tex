\chapter{INTRODUCTION}

In today’s digital marketplace, consumers are exposed to a wide variety of products across multiple brands and categories. While this abundance of choices offers flexibility, it also creates confusion and difficulty in making the right purchase decision. Most buyers rely heavily on price comparison or online reviews, which may not always provide a complete and balanced evaluation of a product’s overall value. Therefore, there is a need for a structured, intelligent, and data-driven system that assists users in selecting the most suitable product based on both quality and cost.

The Smart Price-to-Quality Product Advisor System is designed to address this problem by providing a comprehensive product comparison platform. The system allows users to select a product category, choose preferred brands, and compare multiple products using detailed specifications and features. Instead of focusing only on price, the system calculates a Price-to-Quality Value Score using weighted parameters such as performance, features, user ratings, warranty, and cost. This enables users to make informed and unbiased purchasing decisions.

The platform follows a role-based access model with two primary roles: User and Admin. Users can view, compare, filter, and analyze products but do not have permission to modify product data. The Admin has exclusive access to add new products, edit product details, and delete products from the database. This separation ensures system security, data integrity, and proper management of product information.

Overall, the system aims to simplify the decision-making process, enhance transparency in product evaluation, and provide a reliable solution for students, professionals, and general consumers seeking the best value for their budget.
